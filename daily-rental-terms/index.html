🚫 Cancellation Policy

When you cancel Refund
More than 48 hours before your trip ✅ Full Refund (100%)
24–48 hours before your trip 💳 97% Refund (3% deduction)

🚗 Vehicle Care

Please help us keep our vehicles clean.

  • Avoid carrying pets unless permitted.
  • Do not transport items that may stain, damage, or dirty the vehicle.
  • Any cleaning or repair costs caused during your trip will be charged based on the actual expense.

🔧 Replacement Vehicle

If the vehicle experiences a mechanical breakdown:

  • Within city limits: A replacement vehicle will be provided (subject to availability).
  • Outside city limits: Replacement vehicle is not available.
  • The decision of the company will be final.
