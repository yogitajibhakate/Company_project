ABOUT SUHALAYA

A Synonym for Reliability, Comfort & Hassle-Free Travel

Suhalaya Travels Private Limited is a synonym for reliability, comfort, and a hassle-free experience. As pioneers in corporate car rental services in Bangalore for over two decades, we have been revolutionizing corporate mobility, daily travel, employee transportation, and airport transfers.

Our fleet ranges from economy cabs to luxury sedans and SUVs, with every vehicle undergoing complete detailing every 30 days. Our chauffeurs are well-trained, courteous, experienced, and English-speaking, ensuring utmost client satisfaction on every journey.
