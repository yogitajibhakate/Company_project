🏢 Who We Are

At Suhalaya Travels, your privacy is our priority. We are committed to protecting your personal information and using it responsibly to provide a secure, seamless, and personalized rental experience.

All personal information collected through our website and mobile app is managed by Suhalaya Travels.

Registered Office:

📋 Information We Collect

To process your bookings and improve our services, we may collect:

🚗 Booking Information

  • Full Name
  • Address
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Payment Details (for online payments)

👤 Account Information

If you create a Suhalaya account, we securely store your details to make future bookings faster and more convenient.

⭐ Suhalaya Advantage

If you join our membership program, we'll use your information to manage your membership and provide exclusive offers and benefits.

📄 Booking History

We securely maintain records of your bookings, rental agreements, and transactions for customer support, account management, and legal compliance.

🌐 IP Address

We automatically collect your IP address to understand website traffic, improve performance, and enhance security. Your IP address identifies your Internet Service Provider (ISP) and does not personally identify you.

🍪 Cookies

We use cookies to personalize your experience, remember your preferences, improve website performance, and analyze website usage.

We use both Session Cookies (deleted when you close your browser) and Persistent Cookies (stored until removed). Cookies never damage your device or collect information from other websites.

🔐 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Process and manage your bookings
  • Securely process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website, app, and services
  • Personalize your experience
  • Manage loyalty programs and promotions
  • Comply with legal obligations

🤝 Information Sharing

Your personal information is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. It may only be shared with trusted companies within the Suhalaya Rent A Car Network to support rental operations or when required by law.

🎉 Promotions, Competitions & Surveys

If you participate in promotions or surveys:

  • Basic information may be collected to contact winners or deliver prizes.
  • Survey responses are completely voluntary.
  • Your feedback is used only to improve our services and customer experience.

📩 Email Newsletter

Subscribe to our email newsletter to receive:

  • Exclusive offers 🎁
  • Price updates 💰
  • New services 🚗
  • Promotions and competitions 🏆

We will send newsletters only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe anytime through our website, mobile app, or the unsubscribe link in every email.

✏️ Access or Update Your Information

You may request to view or update your personal information at any time.

📧 Email: marketing@suhalaya.co.in

For security verification, please include:
Full Name, Address, and either your Driving License Number or Date of Birth.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices, or legal requirements.

📢 Any updates will always be published on this page of our website and mobile app.
